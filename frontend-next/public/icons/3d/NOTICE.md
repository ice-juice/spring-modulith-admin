The author's About page states that the initial version is released under
Creative Commons CC0 and is free for personal and commercial projects,
copying, editing, remixing, sharing and redrawing. Only assets listed in the
V1 collection are bundled here; this does not license other premium collections.

These are derived from the author's V1 color/dynamic renders, resized to
128px and encoded as WebP quality 0.88. Where the official transparent PNG
download is available, alpha is preserved; otherwise the border-connected
white background is removed from the official thumbnail. Each file's source,
transformation, original/output SHA256 and size are recorded in manifest.json.
Temporary signed download URLs are not retained. LICENSE.txt is the author's
published V1 license text, fetched from https://3dicons.co/license.md.
Names such as Figma or Blender identify depicted third-party products and
do not imply endorsement or grant trademark rights.
